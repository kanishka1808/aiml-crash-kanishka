# Executive Summary: E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis
**Project Objective:** To identify regional sales patterns, customer behavior, and product trends to drive data-backed business strategy.

---

## 1. Key Analysis Findings
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** The 'health_beauty', 'watches_gifts', and 'bed_bath_table' categories contribute the highest share to overall company revenue.
* **Geographical Hubs:** Sales concentration is heavily skewed towards major metropolitan areas, with **Sao Paulo** and **Rio de Janeiro** acting as the primary revenue generators.
* **Customer Retention:** Over **95%** of the user base consists of one-time buyers, highlighting a critical gap in customer lifetime value (CLV) and retention strategies.
* **Payment Behavior:** **Credit Cards** are the overwhelmingly preferred choice of payment, indicating that customers value credit availability and installment options.
* **Timeline Trends:** Sales show clear cyclicality with structural growth peaks over the quarters, indicating seasonal demand curves.

## 2. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Inventory & Marketing Alignment:** Direct additional marketing spend and optimize supply chains for high-performing categories like Health & Beauty to avoid stockouts.
2. **Targeted Retention Campaigns:** Launch loyalty programs, email remarketing, and personalized discounts targeting one-time buyers to boost the repeat customer rate.
3. **Logistics Optimization:** Establish dedicated fulfillment nodes closer to Sao Paulo and Rio de Janeiro to lower freight times and shipping overheads.
4. **Financial Incentives:** Partner with credit providers to offer exclusive no-cost EMI options or cashback deals to raise average order values (AOV).
